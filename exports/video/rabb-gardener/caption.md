رَبّ — Rabb. Almost always translated as "Lord."

But the root r-b-b doesn't carry a single feudal sound. It means: to grow. To nourish. To raise something stage by stage to the point of its completion.

You weren't created by a king. You were grown by a gardener.

(Lane's Lexicon, Vol. III · Mufradāt al-Rāghib al-Iṣfahānī)
