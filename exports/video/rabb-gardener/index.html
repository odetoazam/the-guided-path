an arabic word
رَبّ
"Rabb."
رَبّ
— translated as —
"Lord"
— but the root doesn't say that.
رَبّ
— what the root means —
the cultivator.
r-b-b — to grow, to nourish, to raise something stage by stage to the point of its completion.
You weren't created by a king.
You were grown by a gardener.
— Lane's Lexicon · Mufradāt al-Rāghib
