إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
Al-Fatiha 1:5
You alone we worship,
You alone we seek help from.
AyahGuide
Normal Arabic word order:
نَعْبُدُكَ
naʿbuduka — we worship You
Ordinary word order. Many possible objects.
إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ
— cleared —
iyyāka — YOU ALONE. Object before verb. Every other object: cleared.
ḤAṢR
نَعْبُدُكَ
verb → object ordinary undirected
إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ
object → verb ḥaṣr exclusive
Fronting the object grammatically excludes all others. Not a style choice. A structural claim.
وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
اسْتَـعِينُ
ista- prefix = actively SEEKING. You must reach.
It happens TWICE. Once for worship. Once for help-seeking.
إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
He recites this at least 17 times a day.
So do you.
The grammar is not poetry. It is structural monotheism.
