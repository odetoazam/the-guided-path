"In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful." (Bismillāh.)

It opens 113 of the Quran's 114 surahs.

Only one breaks the pattern — Surah At-Tawbah (chapter 9). It opens with a declaration of war against treaty-breakers, not a sentence about mercy. The classical scholars saw the absence as the message: this surah's opening could not be in the same breath as Bismillāh.
