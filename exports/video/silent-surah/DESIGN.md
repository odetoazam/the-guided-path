# AyahGuide · The Silent Surah — Design

## Concept
Of the Quran's 114 surahs, 113 begin with the same line — Bismillāh ar-Rahmān ar-Rahīm.
One does not. The visual mechanic is a 114-cell grid where 113 cells light up gold one by one. One stays dark.
Reveal: that one is At-Tawbah — the surah that opens with a declaration of war, not mercy.

## Format
Format 6 (Absent Word). What the Quran *doesn't* do is data. Negative space is the content.

## Style Prompt
Slow, archival, almost forensic. The grid is the visual evidence. Numbers light up like a chart of revelation. Then one cell refuses. The viewer's eye finds the gap before the caption names it.

## Colors
- `#0f172a` — Navy. Background.
- `#f5f1e8` — Cream. Primary text.
- `rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.85)` — Gold. Lit cells, accents, hairlines.
- `rgba(201, 168, 76, 0.18)` — Gold dim. Unlit cell borders, restraint.
- `rgba(245, 241, 232, 0.55)` — Cream muted. Labels, secondary text.

## Typography
- **Amiri** — Arabic Bismillah text. Direction RTL.
- **Playfair Display** — Display headings, the takeaway line.
- **Inter** — Cell numbers (tabular-nums), labels.

## Motion Vocabulary
- Cells light up in a deterministic stagger sweep (top-left → bottom-right) — reads like a chart filling in.
- One cell — At-Tawbah's cell — is conspicuously skipped. The eye finds the void.
- Final reveal pulls focus to that empty cell, surrounding cells dim.
- Climax: the empty cell labels itself.

## What NOT to Do
- No grid randomization — the sweep must feel ordered.
- No celebratory effects on the dark cell. The point is gravity, not victory.
- No "did you know" caption energy — let the visual carry the weight.
- No exclamation points.
