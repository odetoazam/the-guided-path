# Caption — asr-sworn-by-time

The Quran doesn't warn you that you might lose.

It says you're already *inside* loss — right now.

وَٱلْعَصْرِ — "By Time." The root ع-ص-ر means to squeeze. To press. To wring. The oath is sworn by the thing already compressing your life, grain by grain.

إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ

Three layers of emphasis at once — the heaviest Arabic grammar can carry. And that word فِى: it doesn't say "at" loss or "toward" loss. It says you are *in* it. Immersed. Surrounded.

The default state of a human life — by divine diagnosis — is deficit.

Then: إِلَّا

One door. Four conditions:

آمَنُوا — they believed
وَعَمِلُوا ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ — they did repairing deeds (ص-ل-ح = to mend, restore, fix — not just "good")
وَتَوَاصَوْا بِٱلْحَقِّ — they mutually counseled truth
وَتَوَاصَوْا بِٱلصَّبْرِ — they mutually counseled patience

That verb تَوَاصَوْا appears twice. It's Form VI — the grammar of mutual, reciprocal action. Not "I advised you." Not "you told me." *We counseled each other.*

Imam Shafi'i said: if only this surah had been revealed, it would be sufficient for people.

In three verses. Fourteen words. The complete diagnosis of the human condition — and its only cure.
