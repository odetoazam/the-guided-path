"Every soul will taste death." — Āl ʿImrān 3:185

It's one of the most quoted verses in the Quran. But the word it uses isn't "soul."

It's نَفْس — *nafs*.

And nafs doesn't mean some abstract spirit floating somewhere inside you. The root carries *self · breath · person* — the living, breathing, choosing you.

So read it again, properly:

Every *nafs* will taste death.
Not your soul, somewhere out there. YOU. Personally.

It was never distant metaphysics. It was always a direct address — to the one holding the phone right now.

Save this for the next time death feels like something that only happens to other people 🤍
