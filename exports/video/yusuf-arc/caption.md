The Quran's most complete story arc — mapped in four stations.

رُءْيَا → الْجُبّ → السِّجْن → الْخَزَائِن

A dream no one believed.
Cast into a pit by his brothers.
Imprisoned for a crime he didn't commit.
Appointed over all of Egypt.

Every low was a waystation.
Every injustice was a setup.

وَلَا نُضِيعُ أَجْرَ الْمُحْسِنِينَ
"We do not waste the reward of those who do good." — 12:56
