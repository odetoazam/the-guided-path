The Arabic word for "human" (إنسان) shares its root with "to forget" (نَسِيَ).

Two of those letters were already inside the word for human.
The third was always implied.

"We took a promise from Adam — and he forgot." — Ṭā Hā 20:115

Maybe that's why the Quran — all 6,236 ayahs — is the reminder.
