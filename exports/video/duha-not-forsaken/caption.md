Revelation stopped coming. For days.

And the people mocked the Prophet ﷺ: "his Lord has abandoned him."

Then Sūrah Ad-Duḥā came down — and look at *exactly* how Allah answered it:

"Your Lord has not bid you farewell, nor does He detest—" (93:3)

Now look at the Arabic, because something is hidden in it.

On the first verb, He keeps the "you": *waddaʿa-**ka*** — "He has not bid **YOU** farewell."

But on the second verb — "detest" — He drops it. He does not say *qalā-**ka*** ("detest you"). He just says *qalā*.

He would not let the word "detest" sit beside "you" — not even inside a sentence denying it. He spared you from being the object of that word, even grammatically.

That is how gently He told someone who felt abandoned: you were never abandoned.

And then — the promise with no ceiling: "Your Lord is going to keep giving to you, until you are pleased." (93:5)

Save this for a day the sky feels silent 🤍
