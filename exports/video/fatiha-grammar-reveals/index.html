صِرَاطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ
Three groups. Three grammatical voices.
Each one revealing something about divine character.
AyahGuide
Group One
أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ
Active voice.
أَنْعَمْتَ — Form IV perfect, 2nd person singular.
"YOU blessed them." Allah is the named subject.
He takes direct credit
for the good He gives.
AyahGuide
Group Two
ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ
Passive voice.
مَغْضُوب — passive participle (مَفْعُول pattern).
"Wrath was enacted upon them." No actor named.
He does not name Himself
as the source of punishment.
That is divine adab.
AyahGuide
Group Three
ٱلضَّآلِّينَ
Active participle.
ضَالِّينَ — فَاعِل pattern, active.
"Those who went astray." They are the actors.
Not led astray.
Not pushed. They walked off.
Going astray is attributed to the people themselves.
