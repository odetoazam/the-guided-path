Read the opening of Surah Ar-Rahman slowly:

"He taught the Quran." (55:2)
"He created the human." (55:3)

Notice the order. He mentions teaching the Quran *before* creating the human who would read it.

That isn't a mistake. In Arabic, what matters most is placed first. So the sequence is making a quiet, staggering claim:

Your guidance was prepared before you existed. Your rescue was written before your need.

He didn't create you and *then* work out how to help you. The manual was ready before the machine was built — because the surah opens with the name **Ar-Raḥmān**: the One whose mercy overflows, more than can be contained.

So the next time an ayah meets you exactly where you are — don't be surprised. It was placed there before you were.
