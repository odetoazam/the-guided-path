Your heart keeps hunting for calm — in the next achievement, the next purchase, the next scroll. Each one quiets it for a moment. Then the restlessness comes back.

So what actually settles a heart? The Quran names exactly one thing:

أَلَا بِذِكْرِ ٱللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ ٱلْقُلُوبُ
"Truly — in the remembrance of Allah, hearts find rest." (13:28)

Not entertainment. Not achievement. Not distraction. Dhikr — remembrance.

Everything else is a sedative: it numbs the restlessness for an hour. Dhikr is the only thing that actually *settles* the heart — because it's the only thing the heart was built to rest in.

So much of anxiety is the heart looking for its home in the wrong places. This is the address.

Save this for the next restless night 🤍
