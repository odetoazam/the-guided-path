We hear رِزْق (rizq) and think: salary. Income. The number in the account.

But the word was always far wider than money.

The root ر-ز-ق is *every provision that sustains you* — and most of it isn't cash. Your health. The knowledge you carry. A heart that can still feel calm. The people who show up when you're falling. Even your faith itself is a rizq.

And then the promise in Surah At-Talaq:

وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ
"And He provides from where he does not expect." (65:3)

The provision you can't budget for. The one that arrives through a door you'd never have thought to knock on.

Your rizq was measured out before you were born. So chase the means — but don't mistake the salary for the Source.
