## 4. The 12:24 position (grounded, sections verified)

- **al-Qurṭubī**: «ولا خلاف أن همها كان المعصية» — HER hamm: no disagreement. HIS: the
  scholars differed. The taqdīm reading — the lawlā cancels the hamm («لما رأى
  البرهان ما هم») — tied by him to the ʿiṣma of prophets.
- **al-Ṭabarī**: preserves the strong early reports (incl. Ibn ʿAbbās) and answers
  the "how can a prophet…" objection himself: the people of knowledge differed; some
  said prophets are tested with a slip so they remain in awe and never presume.
- **The burhān**: al-Baghawī — Qatāda and "most of the commentators": the image of
  Yaʿqūb (report-genre; say so); Ibn ʿĀshūr sorts the positions into four abstract
  kinds (rational proof / waḥy / divine protection / visions). The Quran itself
  leaves burhān unglossed.
- **The verse's own ending on EVERY reading**: «كذلك لنصرف عنه السوء والفحشاء إنه من
  عبادنا المخلصين» — God turned the evil away; he is of Our chosen servants. Plus the
  mukhlaṣīn/mukhliṣīn qirāʾa (al-Qurṭubī / al-Ṭabarī). The verse ends in acquittal.
- 12:26-27 (the shirt torn from behind + the household witness) and 12:51 («الآن حصحص
  الحق», her own «أنا راودته») are the narrative's own exoneration — use them.
