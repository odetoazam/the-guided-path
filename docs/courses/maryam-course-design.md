## 2. Spine — corpus-verified 2026-08-26

- **Maryam is named in 31 verses** — the only woman the Quran names at all.
- **3:42 contains iṣṭafāki TWICE in one verse**: chosen — purified — chosen above the
  women of the worlds. (al-Ṭabarī glosses the second: *of her time*, with the khayru-nisāʾihā
  hadith; others read absolutely; the four-women hadith (Aḥmad, from Ibn ʿAbbās) sits in the
  66:12 report. M4 names the scope difference.)
- **The command-verdict bracket:** 3:43 **اقنتي** (imperative, at her story's start) →
  66:12 **من القانتين** (verdict, in her LAST mention in the Quran — 66:12 is the highest-numbered verse naming her, verified) — same root, and the
  masculine plural, for which **al-Baghawī** states the point explicitly: «ولذلك لم يقل من
  القانتات» — with ʿAṭāʾ's and the clan readings alongside.
- **ṣ-d-q bracket:** 66:12 **ṣaddaqat** bi-kalimāti rabbihā → 5:75 **ṣiddīqa**. **Al-Saʿdī
  welds it by name**: ṣaddaqat = kamāl al-ʿilm, qānitīn = kamāl al-ʿamal, «فإنها صدِّيقة،
  والصديقية هي كمال العلم والعمل». And 4:171: the *kalima* was cast **to her** — she believed
  the words and was chosen to carry the Word.
- **Divine name: ar-Raḥmān, demonstrated.** Sūrah 19 carries the name **16 times — more
  than double any other sūrah** (runner-up: 43 with 7). Her own first spoken word in her
  ordeal is refuge in it (19:18); her vow of silence is *to ar-Raḥmān* (19:26); the boy is
  given as *raḥma* (19:21).

## 4. M4 table — grounded, sections verified

- **«يا أخت هارون» (19:28):** the polemic was asked in the Prophet's ﷺ own lifetime and
  answered by him — **Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, al-Mughīra b. Shuʿba**, Najrān: «إنهم كانوا يسمون
  بأنبيائهم والصالحين قبلهم». Alongside: ʿAlī b. Abī Ṭalḥa + al-Suddī (of Hārūn's line, like
  "brother of Tamīm"); Qatāda per al-Baghawī (a righteous namesake); Ibn Kathīr ("one
  resembling Hārūn in worship"). al-Qurṭubī opens it «اختلف الناس».
- **Was she a prophetess?** Live ikhtilāf, names on both sides: **al-Qurṭubī rules
  «والصحيح أن مريم نبية»** (waḥy via the angel, as to the prophets); **Ibn ʿĀshūr** allows
  nubūʾa-without-risāla for women. The other side: seeing an angel ≠ prophethood (the
  Companions saw Jibrīl as Diḥya), «وما أرسلنا من قبلك إلا رجالا» limits risāla, and 5:75's
  ṣiddīqa read as her stated rank. Do NOT quantify "majority" — present names and arguments.
- **19:24 «فناداها من تحتها»:** Ibn ʿAbbās, ʿAlqama, al-Ḍaḥḥāk, Qatāda = the angel called;
  a minority = ʿĪsā; **al-Qurṭubī rules «والأول أظهر»**. Present as ruled.
- **«وليس الذكر كالأنثى» (3:36):** most read the middle clauses as God's own parenthesis
  over the mother's words (al-Qurṭubī's grammatical case + the Ibn ʿAbbās qirāʾa); handle
  as such, briefly, in M1.
- **Scope of «نساء العالمين»:** al-Ṭabarī: of her time; others: absolute; the four-women
  hadith. Named, not merged.

## 5. Adab

No husband-figure exists in the Quran's telling at all — do not import Joseph the
carpenter. The slander (4:156) is refuted the way the Quran refutes it: named as بهتان
عظيم and given no repetition. Her cry at 19:23 is handled the way al-Saʿdī handles it.
No age numbers, no betrothal lore, no "40,000 Hārūns" report as fact. The four-women and
khayru-nisāʾihā hadiths are cited to their collections as the reports name them.
