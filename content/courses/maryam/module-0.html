Start Here — The Story

This is the story of Maryam, the mother of ʿĪsā, told simply from beginning to end. She is the only woman the Quran calls by name, and a whole chapter of the Quran carries her name. The Quran tells her story in more than one place, so here the parts are put together in order, and you can know the whole story before we look at any part of it closely.

A promise before she was born

The story begins before Maryam was born. Her mother, the wife of ʿImrān, was expecting a child, and she made a promise to God about the baby she was carrying.

إِذْ قَالَتِ ٱمْرَأَتُ عِمْرَٰنَ رَبِّ إِنِّى نَذَرْتُ لَكَ مَا فِى بَطْنِى مُحَرَّرًا فَتَقَبَّلْ مِنِّىٓ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ

"When the wife of ʿImrān said: My Lord, indeed I have vowed to You what is in my womb, consecrated — so accept it from me. Indeed, You are the Hearing, the Knowing."

Āl ʿImrān (3:35)

She promised that her child would be set apart to serve God alone, and she asked Him to accept her gift.

A girl is born

When the baby came, it was a girl. Her mother told God that she had given birth to a girl, and she named her Maryam. Then she asked God to protect Maryam, and the children Maryam would have, from the accursed Satan.

God accepted the girl with a beautiful acceptance and made her grow up well. Some men argued over which of them would look after her, and they threw their pens to decide it. God placed her in the care of Zakariyyā, who was a prophet.

Food in her room

Maryam had a room set apart for prayer, and each time Zakariyyā came to see her there, something surprised him.

فَتَقَبَّلَهَا رَبُّهَا بِقَبُولٍ حَسَنٍ وَأَنۢبَتَهَا نَبَاتًا حَسَنًا وَكَفَّلَهَا زَكَرِيَّا ۖ كُلَّمَا دَخَلَ عَلَيْهَا زَكَرِيَّا ٱلْمِحْرَابَ وَجَدَ عِندَهَا رِزْقًا ۖ قَالَ يَـٰمَرْيَمُ أَنَّىٰ لَكِ هَـٰذَا ۖ قَالَتْ هُوَ مِنْ عِندِ ٱللَّهِ ۖ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرْزُقُ مَن يَشَآءُ بِغَيْرِ حِسَابٍ

"So her Lord accepted her with good acceptance and caused her to grow in a good manner, and put her in the care of Zakariyyā. Every time Zakariyyā entered upon her in the prayer chamber, he found with her provision. He said: O Maryam, from where is this for you? She said: It is from God. Indeed, God provides for whom He wills without account."

Āl ʿImrān (3:37)

Every time he went in, he found food with her. When he asked her where it came from, she told him it was from God, and that God gives to whoever He wills without counting. Right there, Zakariyyā turned to his Lord and asked Him for a good child of his own.

Chosen by God

Then the angels spoke to Maryam. They told her that God had chosen her and made her pure, and that He had chosen her above the women of the worlds. They told her to be devoutly obedient to her Lord, and to prostrate and bow with those who bow.

The angels also brought her good news of a word from God. His name would be the Messiah, ʿĪsā son of Maryam. He would be honoured in this world and the next, he would be one of those brought near to God, and he would speak to people as a baby in the cradle and as a grown man.

Maryam asked her Lord how she could have a child when no man had touched her. She was told that this is how God creates whatever He wills. When He decides something, He only says to it "Be," and it is.

The messenger

Maryam went away from her family to a place toward the east, and she put up a screen to be alone. Then God sent His Spirit to her, and he appeared to her as a man, well formed in every way. Her first words were a prayer for protection.

قَالَتْ إِنِّىٓ أَعُوذُ بِٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ مِنكَ إِن كُنتَ تَقِيًّا

"She said: Indeed, I seek refuge in the Most Merciful from you, if you should be fearing of God."

Maryam (19:18)

He told her that he was only a messenger from her Lord, sent to give her a pure boy. She asked how she could have a boy when no man had touched her and she had never been unchaste. He answered that her Lord said this was easy for Him, and that the boy would be a sign for people and a mercy from God. It was a matter already decided.

Under the palm tree

So Maryam became pregnant, and she went away with the child to a far place. The Quran does not say how long she carried him. When the pains of birth came, they drove her to the trunk of a palm tree. There she said, "I wish I had died before this, and been forgotten completely."

Then a voice called to her from beneath her. The Quran does not say whose voice it was. It told her not to grieve, because her Lord had placed a stream beneath her. It told her to shake the trunk of the palm tree toward herself, so that fresh ripe dates would fall down to her. She was to eat and drink and be comforted.

The voice also told her what to do if she met anyone. She was to say that she had vowed a fast to the Most Merciful, and so she would not speak to any person that day.

Back to her people

Then she came back to her people, carrying the baby. They said, "O Maryam, you have done a monstrous thing. O sister of Hārūn, your father was not a man of evil, and your mother was not unchaste." Maryam did not answer them. Instead, she pointed to the child.

فَأَشَارَتْ إِلَيْهِ ۖ قَالُوا۟ كَيْفَ نُكَلِّمُ مَن كَانَ فِى ٱلْمَهْدِ صَبِيًّا قَالَ إِنِّى عَبْدُ ٱللَّهِ ءَاتَىٰنِىَ ٱلْكِتَـٰبَ وَجَعَلَنِى نَبِيًّا

"So she pointed to him. They said: How can we speak to one who is in the cradle a child? He said: Indeed, I am the servant of God. He has given me the Scripture and made me a prophet."

Maryam (19:29–30)

The baby went on speaking from the cradle. He said that God had made him blessed wherever he was, and had told him to pray and give zakāh as long as he lived. God had made him good to his mother, and had not made him a proud and wretched tyrant. He ended with these words: "Peace be on me the day I was born, the day I die, and the day I am raised alive."

The Quran does not record a single word Maryam said to defend herself. Her son spoke for her.

How the Quran remembers her

The Quran says that God made the son of Maryam and his mother a sign, and that He gave them both shelter on high ground, a place of rest with flowing water. It also calls Maryam a ṣiddīqa, a woman of truth.

The last verse in the Quran to name her comes near the end of the book. There God gives two women as an example for those who believe. The first is the wife of Pharaoh, and the second is Maryam.

وَمَرْيَمَ ٱبْنَتَ عِمْرَٰنَ ٱلَّتِىٓ أَحْصَنَتْ فَرْجَهَا فَنَفَخْنَا فِيهِ مِن رُّوحِنَا وَصَدَّقَتْ بِكَلِمَـٰتِ رَبِّهَا وَكُتُبِهِۦ وَكَانَتْ مِنَ ٱلْقَـٰنِتِينَ

"And Maryam, the daughter of ʿImrān, who guarded her chastity — so We breathed into [the opening of her garment] of Our Spirit; and she believed in the words of her Lord and His scriptures, and was of the devoutly obedient."

At-Taḥrīm (66:12)

The angels had once told her to be devoutly obedient to her Lord. In this last verse, God says that she was one of the devoutly obedient.
