Start Here — The Story

Before any analysis, you should be able to tell this story out loud, start to finish. One note first. The Quran tells Ādam's story seven times, in seven different chapters, and each telling keeps different pieces. No single chapter has all of it. What follows is assembled from those seven. Module 4 looks at why the story is spread out this way.

An announcement, and an objection

The story begins before the man exists. God tells the angels what He is about to do.

وَإِذْ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةِ إِنِّى جَاعِلٌ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ خَلِيفَةً ۖ قَالُوٓا۟ أَتَجْعَلُ فِيهَا مَن يُفْسِدُ فِيهَا وَيَسْفِكُ ٱلدِّمَآءَ وَنَحْنُ نُسَبِّحُ بِحَمْدِكَ وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَ ۖ قَالَ إِنِّىٓ أَعْلَمُ مَا لَا تَعْلَمُونَ

"And when your Lord said to the angels: Indeed, I will make upon the earth a successive authority. They said: Will You place upon it one who causes corruption in it and sheds blood, while we declare Your praise and sanctify You? He said: Indeed, I know that which you do not know."

Al-Baqarah (2:30)

Two details here matter for the rest of the course. The first is the word for what this creature will be: khalīfa, a steward, a deputy, someone put in charge of a place. The second is that the place is named, and it is the earth. Nothing has gone wrong yet, and there is nothing to punish, but the earth is already the assignment. That is worth remembering when we reach the part people call "the Fall."

The angels then ask an honest question. This creature will corrupt and kill, they say, while we already worship You perfectly. God does not argue with the facts. He answers, I know what you do not know. Some of what He knew, the angels are about to be shown. The rest takes the whole story.

Made, taught, honoured

The man is formed from clay, and the Quran uses several words for its stages, from dust to shaped dark mud. Then something happens that is said nowhere else about anything else. God breathes into him of His spirit, and commands the angels to fall down before him. In Sūrat Ṣād, God says He created him with My two hands. In the Baqarah telling, the new creature is taught something the angels were not, the names, all of them, and when the angels cannot recite what he can, their question is answered in front of them.

All the angels bow. One being present refuses, argues that his own materials are better, and is cast out. That refusal is a whole story of its own, and it has its own course. From here on he appears in this story only where he touches Ādam. What counts here is his exit line. He asks to be left until the Day of Resurrection. He is granted a respite, with its end date set by God rather than by the request, and he promises to come after this creature and its children.

The garden, the warning, the whisper

Ādam and his wife are settled in the garden and told to eat freely from wherever they like, with one tree marked off. The warning is personal and explicit. In Sūrat Ṭā Hā, God tells Ādam by name: this one is an enemy to you and to your wife, so do not let him drive you both out of the garden.

The enemy then goes to work, and he uses a whisper rather than force. In the Ṭā Hā telling he comes to Ādam with a question: shall I show you the tree of eternity, and a kingdom that never fades? In al-Aʿrāf he works on the two of them together and re-explains the prohibition, and he swears an oath to them both that he is a sincere adviser. In Ṭā Hā, the Quran sets up the moment with one very gentle sentence about a human being.

وَلَقَدْ عَهِدْنَآ إِلَىٰٓ ءَادَمَ مِن قَبْلُ فَنَسِىَ وَلَمْ نَجِدْ لَهُۥ عَزْمًا

"And We had already taken a promise from Ādam before, but he forgot; and We found not in him determination."

Ṭā Hā (20:115)

He forgot, and his resolve was not firm. Module 2 works through that sentence closely.

The eating, and the two sentences

They eat. What was promised, immortality and a kingdom that never ends, does not arrive. What arrives is exposure. They see themselves, and they start fastening leaves over their bodies. The Quran then says it plainly: and Ādam disobeyed his Lord, and went astray. Module 4 takes that sentence as it stands, because the Quran chose it.

Then comes the fork that this course and its companion course both turn on. Ādam and his wife say something, together, in one breath.

قَالَا رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَآ أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ ٱلْخَـٰسِرِينَ

"They said: Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."

Al-Aʿrāf (7:23)

Al-Baqarah says where that sentence came from. Ādam received words from his Lord, and then God turned to him. He did not compose his own repentance. He was handed it, by the One he had disobeyed. Module 5 is about that.

Down, with a promise

They leave the garden for the earth, the place a khalīfa was always going to stand. It is worth reading what the descent actually carries. There is an enmity clause, some of you enemies to some, and then, over it, this.

قُلْنَا ٱهْبِطُوا۟ مِنْهَا جَمِيعًا ۖ فَإِمَّا يَأْتِيَنَّكُم مِّنِّى هُدًى فَمَن تَبِعَ هُدَاىَ فَلَا خَوْفٌ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا هُمْ يَحْزَنُونَ

"We said: Go down from it, all of you. And when guidance comes to you from Me — whoever follows My guidance, there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve."

Al-Baqarah (2:38)

In the Ṭā Hā telling, one more verse closes the story over his head. His Lord chose him, turned to him, and guided him. The choosing comes after the wrong, not before it. The order of those words gets a module of its own.

The course map

  • Module 0 — Start Here (you are here). The whole story, in order, assembled honestly.
  • Module 1 — Made, and Taught. Before the fall: what kind of creature this is, what the honour was actually for, and what the angels could and could not see.
  • Module 2 — The Whisper and the Forgetting. The deception step by step, the door it came through, and the door out.
  • Module 3 — Two Sentences. Two beings break one command. One says we wronged ourselves and one says You made me stray, and the Quran carries the whole difference in the grammar of a single root.
  • Module 4 — What the Quran Won't Tell You. The things left out: no name for the woman, no species for the tree, no serpent, no inherited guilt. Also the hard verse, with the scholars' views set out.
  • Module 5 — The Words You Were Given. The ending that faces you. The way back was never something you had to invent.

One sentence to carry in. This is the only story in the Quran where the first wrong a human being did is followed straight away by the first mercy a human being was shown. That mercy arrived as words.
