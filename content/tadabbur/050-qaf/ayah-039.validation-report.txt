ISSUES:
- [MODERATE] "what the grammarians call fāʾ as-sababiyyah or the fā of consequence" — Technically mislabeled. The fā in this position is more accurately classified by classical grammarians as fāʾ al-faṣīḥa (the explanatory/elliptical fā), which presupposes a suppressed conditional: "if the situation is as just described, then — so be patient." Fāʾ al-sababiyya (causative fā) is a distinct construction that introduces result clauses after negations, interrogatives, or optative imperatives (e.g., imperatives of wish/hope). The functional reading offered — that the fā creates a consequential link to the preceding argument — is sound and consistent with how tafsir treats this command, but presenting "sababiyya" as the grammarians' term for this specific fā is a technical error. The interpretive point stands; the label does not.
