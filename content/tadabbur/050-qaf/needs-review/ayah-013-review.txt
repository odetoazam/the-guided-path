VERDICT: CRITICAL

ISSUES:

1. **CRITICAL — miscount of the nations listed (seven vs. eight).** The text states *"Seven communities. Seven dismissals. One verdict."* and the frontmatter `passage_context` says *"Three of the seven communities are compressed into this single ayah."* But 50:12–14 names **eight** communities: (1) قوم نوح, (2) أصحاب الرس, (3) ثمود, (4) عاد, (5) فرعون, (6) إخوان لوط, (7) أصحاب الأيكة, (8) قوم تبع. This is a verifiable factual error about the Quranic text itself, stated prominently and in the frontmatter. It also creates an internal contradiction: the later section correctly lists **seven collectives** and treats **Pharaoh as the eighth/exceptional** entry ("every one is a multitude… except one"), which only works if the total is eight. The "Seven communities. Seven dismissals" flourish must be corrected to eight (or rephrased), and `passage_context` fixed.

2. **MODERATE — lexical claim stated as fact.** *"The plural form **ikhwān** is specifically the warm plural: it's used not for organizational membership but for affective closeness."* The إخوة/إخوان distinction is genuine but not this clean — إخوان is also used generically for brothers, and the Quran uses إخوة affectively for believers (49:10 *إنما المؤمنون إخوة*). The intimacy reading is a defensible tadabbur reflection (and is elsewhere appropriately hedged with "invites us to consider"), but the bolded sentence presents an interpretive nuance as settled lexical fact. Soften to interpretive framing.

Note (not flagged): root أخ → أ-خ-و and 79:24 (*أنا ربكم الأعلى*) attribution are correct; al-Muyassar/al-Jalalayn confirm the bare referent (قوم لوط), and the qawm-vs-ikhwān contrast at 7:80 / 27:54 / 29:28 is accurate.
