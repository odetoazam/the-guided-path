- [MODERATE] "it is still hungry. It has not been satisfied. It wants more." — The opening hook presents the *haqīqī istifhām* reading (Hell genuinely asking for more souls) as the established meaning of هَلْ مِن مَّزِيدٍ. Al-Jalālayn explicitly reads this as *istifhām inkārī*: «أي لا أسع غير ما امتلأت به، أي قد امتلأت» — i.e., Hell is declaring *"I am full, I cannot hold more,"* not expressing hunger. Al-Baghawī records the same opinion: «قيل: معناه قد امتلأت». The concepts list correctly flags "istifham inkari vs istifham haqiqi," which confirms the author knows the dispute — but that awareness does not reach the prose. Because the hook is the interpretive frame the entire reflection will build on, resolving this silently in one direction against Jalālayn's dominant reading is an overstatement. (Note: Al-Muyassar does support the insatiability reading via the hadith sequence — «هل من زيادة من الجن والإنس؟ فيضع الرب قدمه فيها... وتقول قط قط» — so the reading is not fabricated, but it is one of two well-attested positions.)
