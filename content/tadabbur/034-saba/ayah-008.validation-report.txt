ISSUES:
- [MODERATE] "It does not ask *whether*. It asks *which*. It assumes the speaker has already accepted that one of the options is true. The only thing left to decide is which one." — The first two sentences accurately characterize أم المعادلة in classical Arabic grammar (confirmed by al-Qurtubi: لما دخلت ألف الاستفهام استغنيت عن ألف الوصل فحذفتها, and al-Baghawi: ألف استفهام دخلت على ألف الوصل). But the third and fourth sentences overstate the case: classical grammarians describe this construction as presenting two open alternatives for the listener to pick between — not as structurally presupposing one is already accepted as true. The false-dichotomy argument is a valid rhetorical observation about the Meccans' intent, but it should be framed as what they are *doing with* the construction, not as a property *of* the construction itself.
