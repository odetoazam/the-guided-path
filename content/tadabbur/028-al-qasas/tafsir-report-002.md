## 28:2

### Ibn Kathir (English)

Imam Ahmad bin Hanbal, may Allah have mercy on him, recorded that Ma'diykarib said: "We came to 'Abdullah and asked him to recite to us:

طسم

(Ṭa Sīn Mīm.) the two hundred. He said, 'I do not know it; you should go to someone who learned it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ Khabbab bin Al-Aratt.' So we went to Khabbab bin Al-Aratt and he recited it to us, may Allah be pleased with him."

### al-Tabari (Arabic)

القول في تأويل قوله جل ثناؤه وتقدست أسماؤه: ﴿طسم (١) تِلْكَ آيَاتُ الْكِتَابِ الْمُبِينِ (٢) نَتْلُوا عَلَيْكَ مِنْ نَبَإِ مُوسَى وَفِرْعَوْنَ بِالْحَقِّ لِقَوْمٍ يُؤْمِنُونَ (٣) ﴾
