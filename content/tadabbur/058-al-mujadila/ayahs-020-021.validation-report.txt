- [MODERATE] Frontmatter comment: `"emphatic لَأَغْلِبَنَّ (lām al-ibtidāʾ + nūn al-tawkīd al-thaqīla) are morphologically secure"` — The lam here is **lām al-qasam** (oath-response lam), not lām al-ibtidāʾ. Classical grammarians (Sībawayhi, Ibn ʿAqīl) are unanimous: when a lam precedes a verb with nūn al-tawkīd, it is the lam of the oath response (jawāb al-qasam). Lām al-ibtidāʾ introduces nominal sentences and does not co-occur with nūn al-tawkīd in verbal sentences. Flagging this as "morphologically secure" under the wrong category is a technical misclassification — though it does not affect the interpretive weight of the emphasis, since both produce the same emphatic force.
