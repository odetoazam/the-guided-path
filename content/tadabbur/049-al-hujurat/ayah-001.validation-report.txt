- [MODERATE] "Do not put forward... Put forward *what?*... Allah does not say what... the dropping of the object opens the meaning to *everything*" — The reflection builds its entire linguistic centerpiece on the claim that تُقَدِّمُوا is a transitive verb with a dramatically withheld object. Al-Baghawi directly contradicts this: he states the majority reading (tuqaddimū, Form II, ḍamm of tāʾ, kasra of dāl) is **lāzim bi-maʿnā al-taqaddum** — *intransitive*, meaning simply "to advance/go ahead." The word-by-word reference corroborates this: "put (yourselves) forward" — the implicit object is reflexive, not missing. Al-Jalālayn also glosses it as "lā taqaddamū" (intransitive advance). The breadth of prohibition (covering word and deed) is real and supported by the tafsir sources, but the mechanism the reflection uses to derive it is grammatically inaccurate.
