- [MODERATE] "Allah had the plain word for safety available. "Three words later" is factually incorrect and verifiable by any reader with the Arabic text open. The underlying linguistic point is valid; only the count is wrong.

- [MODERATE] "It is the kind of form Arabic reserves for actions of trembling, of shaking, and then of coming to rest." — This presents a categorical semantic function for Form XII as a whole, which is not a standard morphological claim. Form XII (اِفْعَلَلَّ / iṭmaʾanna pattern) is described in grammars as an **intensive augmented form**; the "trembling and settling" gloss is derived from this specific root (ṭ-m-n), not from Form XII as a class. Presenting it as a general formal property of Form XII overstates the morphological claim. The phenomenological description is evocative but should be framed as belonging to this root's meaning, not to the form-pattern itself.
