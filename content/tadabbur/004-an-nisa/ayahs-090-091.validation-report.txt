VERDICT: MODERATE

ISSUES:

- [MODERATE] "the act of keeping family bonds alive that the Prophet ﷺ said is hung onto the very Throne" — The Throne-hanging hadith (Bukhārī 5988, Muslim 2554) says *al-raḥimu muʿallaqa bil-ʿarsh* — it is **al-raḥim** (the kinship bond itself, personified) that is suspended from the Throne and cries out. The *act* (ṣila) is what one performs in response to its call. The reflection inverts the subject: it is not ṣilat al-raḥim that "is hung onto the Throne" but al-raḥim. The spirit is sound; the precise attribution of what the hadith places on the Throne is not.
