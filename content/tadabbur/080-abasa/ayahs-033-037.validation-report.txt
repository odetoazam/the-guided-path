- [MODERATE] "the form **صَآخَّة** — is an intensive pattern, the kind of form Arabic reserves for something whose very nature, whose *profession*, is to do this" — Morphologically inaccurate. **صَآخَّة** is a feminine active participle (*ism fāʿil*, pattern *fāʿilah*) from the doubled root ص-خ-خ, not an intensive/mubalāghah form. Intensive patterns in Arabic are *faʿʿāl*, *faʿūl*, *mifʿāl*, *faʿīl*, etc. The *fāʿilah* pattern in this position (naming an eschatological event, cf. الحاقة، الطامة، القارعة) does carry agent-noun force — "the Deafening One" — but that semantic weight flows from its use as a title noun, not from a morphological intensification pattern. The claim as written is a teachable error that a reader with any Arabic training will flag.
