ISSUES:
- [MODERATE] "it is in the intensive-transitive form the grammarians call Form IV" — Form IV (afʿala) is the **causative** form, not the intensive form. Intensive is Form II (faʿʿala — e.g., *nazzala*). Both roots exist in the Quran for n-z-l: *nazzala* (Form II, repeated/intensive descent) vs. *anzala* (Form IV, causative dispatch). The verse uses Form IV correctly, and the functional gloss ("deliberate, directed bringing-down," "a King dispatching") is accurate — only the label "intensive" is wrong and would mislead a reader trying to learn morphology. A tadabbur audience building on this will carry forward a false equation of Form IV = intensity.
