Al-Baghawi's own tafsir on this very verse cites 2:20 ("ولو شاء الله لذهب بسمعهم وأبصارهم") as a direct structural parallel, noting "كما قال الله عز وجل" — "as Allah said." The counterfactual-withholding construction is not singular to 36:66. The reflection even self-lists 2:20 as a *related_ayah*, making this internally inconsistent.

- [MODERATE] "the present of renewal — the sustaining is happening continuously... a loan being re-extended, moment by moment" — *nashāʾu* is indeed mudāriʿ (imperfect/present), but the theological elaboration of *continuous moment-by-moment renewal* is an interpretive extension not supported by any of the four tafsir sources provided. None of al-Jalalayn, al-Muyassar, al-Qurtubi, or al-Baghawi draw attention to the verb tense as a carrier of this meaning. The concept is plausible and coheres with broader Islamic theology (*tawakkul*, divine sustaining), but it is presented as a linguistic derivation ("what the linguists would call") when no classical lexical source is cited for it.

The text cuts before the full *ṭamasnā* analysis, but the direction taken — distinguishing *ṭams* from ordinary blindness (*aʿmā*) — is well-grounded. Al-Qurtubi explicitly states: "المطموس والطميس عند أهل اللغة الأعمى الذي ليس في عينيه شق," and al-Baghawi: "بحيث لا يبدو لها جفن ولا شق." That element is on solid footing.
