## 32:13

### Ibn Kathir (English)

Verily, we now believe with certainty. (12)And if We had willed, surely We would have given every person his guidance, but the Word from Me took effect, that I will fill Hell with Jinn and mankind together (13)Then taste because of your forgetting the meeting of this Day of yours.
