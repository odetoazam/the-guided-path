The construction *tāba ʿalā* with Allah as subject (Allah "turning toward" someone) is not unique to this verse; it appears multiple times in the Quran — e.g., 2:37 (*fa-tāba ʿalayhi wa-hadāhu*), 9:117, 9:118 — in contexts where the believers are also in a state of trial or remorse, not merely "sin."
