- [MODERATE] "The root pulses three times in one short phrase, each time in a new costume" — The root ض ع ف appears **twice** in the phrase يُضَاعَفْ لَهَا الْعَذَابُ ضِعْفَيْنِ: once in the Form III passive verb يُضَاعَفْ and once in the dual noun ضِعْفَيْنِ. There is no third occurrence. The author appears to have conflated ضِعْفَيْنِ's dual *semantics* (representing two instances of ضِعْف) with two separate morphological root appearances — a category error. The rhetorical point about polyptoton still holds, but the count is wrong and the linguistic credibility of that section rests on precision.

- [MINOR] "ضِعْفَيْنِ — *two-fold, a double dual*" — "double dual" is non-standard and potentially misleading. ضِعْفَيْنِ is the ordinary dual of ضِعْف (itself meaning "a fold/double"); calling it a "double dual" implies a dual-of-a-dual construction (which would mean four-fold). The correct description is "dual form of ضِعْف." The word-by-word reference simply glosses it "two fold," which is all that is warranted.
