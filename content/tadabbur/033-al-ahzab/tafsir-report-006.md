## 33:6

### Ibn Kathir (English)

The Prophet is closer to the believers than themselves, and his wives are their mothers. And blood relations among each other have closer personal ties in the decree of Allah than the believers and the Muhajirin, except that you do kindness to those brothers. This has been written in the Book (6)

Loyalty to the Prophet ﷺ; and his wives are Mothers of the Believers

Allah tells us how His Messenger ﷺ is merciful and sincere towards his Ummah, and how he is closer to them than they are to them...
