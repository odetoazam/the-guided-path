- [CRITICAL] "In Sūrat al-Ḥāqqah, the very nation we are discussing is destroyed by *rīḥin ṣarṣarin ʿātiyah* — a screaming wind that is **ʿātiyah**, from this same root" — This is a nation inversion. In al-Ḥāqqah, **Thamud** (the nation under discussion) is destroyed by *al-ṭāghiyah* (69:5: فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ). The *rīḥin ṣarṣarin ʿātiyah* — the screaming ʿātiyah wind — is the fate of **ʿĀd**, not Thamud (69:6: وَأَمَّا عَادٌ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِرِيحٍ صَرْصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٍ). The etymological link between ʿātiyah and ʿataw via root ʿ-T-W is lexically sound, but as written the reflection hands a reader a false Quranic fact: Thamud was not destroyed by the ʿātiyah wind. The argument must either be reframed around ʿĀd or dropped.
