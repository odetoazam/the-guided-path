- [CRITICAL] "This is the final verse of Surah Ad-Dukhan" / "the surah closes not on a threat but on a gift: We made this speech easy upon your tongue" — Factually wrong. Surah Ad-Dukhan has **59 verses**, not 58. The actual closing verse is 44:59: *فَارْتَقِبْ إِنَّهُم مُّرْتَقِبُونَ* ("So wait — indeed they are waiting"), which is a warning to the Prophet and a veiled threat to the disbelievers. Al-Qurtubi's tafsir provided in the reference data quotes **both verses together** in sequence: "فإنما يسرناه بلسانك لعلهم يتذكرون **فارتقب إنهم مرتقبون**" — making explicit that 44:58 is the penultimate verse, not the final one. The entire introductory theological argument ("Allah does not close with a final threat... He closes with this...") is inverted: the surah actually *does* close with a warning-adjacent verse. The ring-composition claim linking the surah's opening to its "closing" via 44:58 is therefore structurally unsupported.
