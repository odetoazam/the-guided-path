The word الْحِمَارِ (donkey) derives from the root ح-م-ر (ḥ-m-r), an entirely separate root — as the reflection's *own frontmatter* correctly distinguishes: "Hml حمل, Hmr حمر." The body text contradicts its own morphology note.

- [MODERATE] "al-Qurṭubī even records a second shade from al-Jurjānī: it carries the sense of *kafāla,* of taking on a guarantee, a sacred liability" — The Qurṭubī excerpt provided reads only "وَقَالَ الجُرجاني : هو مِن الحمَالة بم" and is **truncated**. الحمالة can mean guarantee/surety, which is consistent with the kafāla gloss, but the full sentence is cut off. This claim cannot be confirmed as stated from the provided data. It should be hedged as a plausible reading of a fragment, not a confirmed attribution.
