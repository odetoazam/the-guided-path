- [MODERATE] "A *jasad*, here, is precisely a body that **does not eat** — a form without the living functions of a living being." — The logic from 21:8 is inverted. The verse reads *"wa-mā jaʿalnāhum jasadan lā yaʾkulūna l-ṭaʿām"* — "We did not make them a jasad that does not eat." The verse DENIES that the prophets were non-eating bodies, confirming they were normal living humans. The semantic weight of "doesn't eat / lifeless form" comes from the relative clause *lā yaʾkulūna*, not from *jasad* alone. The reflection extracts *jasad* in isolation and says it "is precisely a body that does not eat" — that attribution belongs to the compound phrase, not the root.
