- [MODERATE] "al-Rāghib al-Iṣfahānī in *Mufradāt alfāẓ al-Qurʾān* notes that naba' carries what khabar does not require: it concerns something of great significance, it comes from a reliable source, and **it carries consequence for the one who receives it**" — The Mufradāt is not among the provided reference sources, so full verification is impossible here. However, al-Rāghib's actual formulation defines naba' as خبر ذو فائدة عظيمة يحصل به علم أو غلبة ظن (news of great utility that produces knowledge or strong probability), with conditions of great importance and truthfulness — not "consequence for the recipient" as a discrete third criterion. "Consequence" is the reflection author's interpretive gloss presented as al-Rāghib's stated condition. The naba'/khabar distinction itself is sound, but the third criterion is an extension attributed to al-Rāghib that he does not explicitly state in those terms.
