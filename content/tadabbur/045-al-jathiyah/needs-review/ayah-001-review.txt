VERDICT: CRITICAL

ISSUES:
- **CRITICAL — wrong Quranic citation:** "a book that says, explicitly: *if this is human speech, produce something like it* (41:13)." Q 41:13 (Fuṣṣilat) is *not* the challenge verse — it reads "But if they turn away, then say: I have warned you of a thunderbolt (ṣāʿiqa) like the thunderbolt of ʿĀd and Thamūd." The taḥaddī ("produce something like it") verses are 2:23, 10:38, 11:13, 17:88, 52:34 — not 41:13. This is a factual misattribution presented as an explicit quotation and must be corrected (likely intended 2:23 or 10:38).

- **MODERATE — attribution stated as fact:** "The Kufan grammarians saw them as *fawātiḥ* — attention-capture devices." *Fawātiḥ al-suwar* simply means "openings of the surahs" (a descriptive category), not specifically a Kufan-grammarian doctrine of attention-capture. The "attention-capture / istiftāḥ" reading is one interpretive view but the specific ascription to "the Kufan grammarians" is not well-grounded and should be softened or sourced.

- Note (not blocking): The composite "al-Jalālayn, al-Muyassar, al-Baghawī, al-Qurṭubī … the conclusion is the same: 'Allah knows best'" is defensible — al-Jalālayn's wording (اللَّه أعْلَم بِمُرادِهِ بِهِ) is confirmed in the tafsir report — but al-Qurṭubī in fact canvasses many views at length before tafwīḍ; the sketch flattens him slightly. Acceptable, but tighten if convenient.

All factual claims about the Ḥawāmīm sequence (surahs 40–46, 45 as the sixth, 42 adding ʿAyn Sīn Qāf, 29 muqaṭṭaʿāt surahs, Meccan provenance, surah-by-surah themes) are accurate.
