[5] Claude semantic check:
VERDICT: MODERATE

ISSUES:
- [MODERATE] "the animal that flattens its ears back against its skull and braces and will not, *will not*, move" — Qurṭubī's text reads: **"وهو أن ينحني عليها صارا أذنيه"** — the donkey *presses/leans forward toward* the herd of she-donkeys (العانة) while pinning its ears. The reflection inverts the directionality: Qurṭubī's image is of forward-pressing, dogged pursuit — not a creature bracing against movement. The ears detail is accurate; the "refuses to move" gloss is not.

- [MODERATE] "*Yuṣirru* sits in the fourth pattern — *ifʿāl*, *iṣrār* — the pattern that here carries the sense of **entering fully into a state and locking into it**" — the Form IV identification is morphologically correct (أَصَرَّ → يُصِرُّ, maṣdar إصرار). However, the specific semantic gloss "entering fully into a state and locking into it" is an editorial morphological claim presented as established fact. None of the four provided tafsir sources use this framing; Jalalayn, Muyassar, and Qurṭubī all gloss *yuṣirru* as *yatamādā ʿalā kufrih* (continues/persists in his disbelief), not a Form IV state-entry semantics. The claim is defensible but exceeds what the provided reference data supports.

The coin-purse etymology (*maʾkhūdh min ṣarr al-ṣurra idhā shaddahā*) is accurately quoted and correctly attributed to Qurṭubī. The Makkan night-listening narrative is a recognized sīra account (Ibn Isḥāq) and is presented as contextual backdrop, not an exegetical claim — this is acceptable framing.

SUMMARY: Two moderate issues — the donkey image mischaracterizes Qurṭubī's directionality (pressing-toward, not bracing-away), and the Form IV semantic gloss is asserted without support from the provided sources; no critical errors in Arabic text, root analysis, or scholar attribution.
