CONFIRMED ACCURATE (for completeness):

Pattern is *yaftaʿilu* with the Form VIII *tāʾ* inserted after the first radical (س), yielding deliberate/attentive listening vs. plain Form I *yasmaʿu*. The semantic characterization (reflexive, effortful, self-directed) is linguistically grounded.
- Ibn Masʿūd and Ibn ʿAbbās as recipients of the question: Confirmed by al-Jalalayn ("لعلماء الصحابة منهم ابن مسعود وابن عباس").
