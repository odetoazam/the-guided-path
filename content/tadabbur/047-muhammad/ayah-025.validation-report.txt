ISSUES:
- [MODERATE] "Qatādah and Ibn Jurayj said: these are the People of the Book, who found the description of the Prophet ﷺ written in what they already had, **recognized him the way you recognize your own child**, and then denied him" — The "like your own child" simile is not from Qatādah's recorded statement on this verse. Qurtubi and Baghawi document Qatādah only saying: *كفروا بالنبي صلى الله عليه وسلم بعدما عرفوا نعته عندهم* ("they disbelieved in the Prophet after they knew his description among them"). The "recognize your own child" imagery belongs to 2:146 (*يَعْرِفُونَهُۥ كَمَا يَعْرِفُونَ أَبْنَآءَهُمْ*), a different verse. Presenting it here as part of Qatādah's reading of 47:25 — without signaling the cross-verse borrowing — overstates the documented attribution.
