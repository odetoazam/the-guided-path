- [MODERATE] "across this entire Day, from every possible direction, the question does not come" — The passive-voice analysis is grammatically sound, but this phrasing presents the non-asking as absolute and Day-wide. Jalalayn explicitly reconciles the apparent contradiction with 15:92 by noting "ويُسألون في وقت آخر" (they are asked at another time), and Qurtubi gives the standard mawāṭin resolution: "القيامة مواطن لطول ذلك اليوم، فيسأل في بعض ولا يسأل في بعض" — the Day has multiple stations; questioning occurs at some, not at others. The word "cancellation" compounds this — what the verse describes is a suspension at one station, not an erasure across the whole Day. The author signals an incoming resolution ("stay with me") but the linguistic framing here is stated definitively enough that a reader could absorb it as the settled meaning before the resolution arrives.

This is an interpretive extension grounded in etymology, not a tafsir consensus on how *dhanb* functions in 55:39 specifically. It belongs under "some lexicographers note" rather than as a standalone meaning.
