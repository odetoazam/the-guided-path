### PART 1 — Fixes

**MODERATE #1 — *Min qablu* / dropped tafsir referent**

- **Frontmatter `translation`** (line 10): Removed the interpretive gloss "by separating him from." Changed to "And We aided him against the people who denied Our signs" — the standard rendering that aligns with al-Ṭabarī's own paraphrase (*naṣarnā Nūḥan ʿalā al-qawm*). The body's Element Three then earns the *min* observation by analysis.
- **Body block-quote translation** (line 36): Same change.
- **Introduction** (lines 47–50): Replaced "The Arabic leaves it open. *Min qablu* — 'before,' with no specified anchor." with the actual classical reading: al-Ṭabarī and al-Muyassar both specify *"before you, O Muḥammad — and before Ibrāhīm and Lūṭ."* The consolatory address to the Prophet is named explicitly.
- **Element One** (lines 85–93): Replaced the three speculative "camera angles" (none attributed to a named scholar) with the tafsir-grounded reading first — al-Ṭabarī's and al-Muyassar's *"min qablika wa-min qabli Ibrāhīm wa-Lūṭ"* — and then explained how the *bināʾ ʿalā al-ḍamm* opens the referent wider than its primary anchors without abandoning them.

**MODERATE #2 — *Karb* definition outpacing al-Ṭabarī**

- The Rāghib/*Lisān al-ʿArab* chest-breath analysis is preserved (correctly attributed). Added al-Muyassar's *al-ghamm al-shadīd* gloss alongside it (line 117), grounding the passage in the provided sources. Added al-Ṭabarī's significant alternative reading that *al-karb al-ʿaẓīm* in this verse refers to the catastrophic punishment of the flood itself — not only Nūḥ's interior suffering — and wove both readings together rather than choosing between them (lines 163–164).

**MODERATE #3 — *Naṣara-min* "by separating him from" as definitional**

- Element Three (line 183): Added that al-Ṭabarī himself paraphrases the verse using *ʿalā* ("over/against") in his own Arabic summary, and that most translations do the same — establishing the standard reading *first* before the *min* exploration begins.
- Element Three (line 189): Changed "means: **We gave him victory by separating him from the people**" to "opens an interpretive dimension... **One reading of this preposition is that** the victory and the separation are the same act" — explicitly flagged as interpretive throughout.

### PART 2 — Deepening from Tafsir

1. **Nūḥ's preserved prayers** (line 97) — al-Ṭabarī and Ibn Kathīr both quote 54:10 ("My Lord, I am overcome, so help me") and 71:26 ("My Lord, leave not upon the earth any of the disbelievers") when commenting on *idh nādā*. Added: the design choice of preserving the *act* here vs. the *words* in other surahs is itself the teaching.

2. **"Ahl" = believers, not blood** (line 155) — al-Ṭabarī specifies *ahl al-īmān min waladihi wa-ḥalā'ilihim* ("the believers among his children and their wives"). Added immediately before the Form II *najjā* analysis, which gives the concept tag `ahl-as-belief-bonded-family` its substance in the body.

3. **Al-Muyassar's *min kayd al-qawm*** (line 213) — added alongside the existing *manaʿnāhu* gloss: the deniers were not merely passive but actively scheming, and al-Muyassar's "aided him from the *scheming* of the people" captures this.

4. **Al-Ṭabarī's *qawm sawʾin* definition** (lines 325–328) — added his explanation that they were people who "commit evil deeds, disobey Allah, and oppose His command" (active moral transgression, not just theological error), plus al-Muyassar's *ahl qubḥ*. Added the grammatical observation: the verdict (*innahum kānū qawma sawʾin*) precedes the flood (*fa-aghraqnāhum*) — the moral label comes first; the water follows as consequence.
