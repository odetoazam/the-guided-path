- [MODERATE] "There is *khalaqa* — to create, to bring out of nothing" — The lexical definition of *khalaqa* does not specifically mean ex nihilo creation. The Quran uses *khalaqa* for humans made from clay, from a fluid, from a clot — none of which are "from nothing." The ex nihilo association is a kalam theological doctrine, not the lexical range of *khalaqa* itself. The contrast with *jaʿala* as purposive-appointment is accurate and well-grounded; the pedagogical gloss on *khalaqa* overshoots the lexical claim into theology. This is a moderate issue because it conflates lexical meaning with doctrinal inference, and a reader might incorrectly conclude that every Quranic use of *khalaqa* implies creation ex nihilo.
