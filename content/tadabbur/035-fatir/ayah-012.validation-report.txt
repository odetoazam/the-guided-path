VERDICT: MODERATE

ISSUES:
- [MODERATE] "from each of them, you eat the same fresh fish flesh. From each comes ornament" — The reflection presents ornament extraction from both seas as established fact. Baghawi is explicit: *"من المالح دون"* (from the salt water, not the fresh). Jalalayn gives the same as the primary reading: *"تستخرجون من الملح، وقيل منهما"* (from salt; some say from both). The Quranic *min kullin* is ambiguous enough that the ẓāhir reading supports both seas, but stating it flatly without acknowledging the stronger classical restriction overstates the position. This is the one place the reflection should hedge: "the ẓāhir reading suggests both; classical scholars debate whether this applies to ornaments."

SUMMARY: Morphology and structural analysis are sound throughout; the single issue is presenting ornament extraction from both seas as settled when the dominant classical position (Baghawi, Jalalayn) restricts ḥilya to the salt water.
