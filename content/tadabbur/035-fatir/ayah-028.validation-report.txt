- [MODERATE] "Allah did not say mukhtalafun — 'differentiated.' That would have been the passive — they were made to differ — implying an external imposition of difference upon a base of sameness... It means: the diversity of these creatures is not painted on top of a base sameness. The diversity is in their nature itself." — The linguistic distinction between active and passive participles is real and the Form VIII identification is correct. However, this theological inference — that the active form *proves* diversity is intrinsically embedded rather than externally imposed — is stated as linguistic fact when it is an interpretive extension. None of the four provided tafsir sources (Jalalayn, Muyassar, Qurtubi, Baghawi) make this active/passive distinction or derive this conclusion from the participle form. Qurtubi's comment on *kadhālik* is the closest analog ("likewise the states of servants differ in their fear"), but that supports the structural argument, not the intrinsic-diversity claim. This should be framed as a reflection observation, not a linguistic determination.
