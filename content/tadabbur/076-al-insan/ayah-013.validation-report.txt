ISSUES:

- [MODERATE] "The lexicons and every one of our commentators agree: the bitter, biting, intense cold" — This overstates the consensus. Jalālayn presents the cold reading first (*lā ḥarran wa-lā bardan*) but then explicitly records a second opinion: *"wa-qīla az-zamharīru al-qamar"* — "and it is said zamharīr means the moon, so paradise is illuminated without sun or moon." The reflection's claim of unanimity across all four commentators is inaccurate; Jalālayn transmits a recognized minority tafsir position that the word refers to the moon, not cold.

CONFIRMED ACCURATE (explicit reference verification):

- The identification of *muttaki'īn* as Form VIII (*iftaʿala*, *ittaka'a → muttaki'*) from root و-ك-أ is linguistically correct.
- The ḥāl analysis — that *muttaki'īn* is governed by *jazāhum* not *ṣabarū* — is confirmed verbatim by Qurṭubī (*"والعامل فيها جزى ولا يعمل فيها صبروا"*) and by Baghawī (*"نصب على الحال"*).

- Zamharīr = severe cold is confirmed by Baghawī (*"البرد الشديد"*) and Muyassar (*"شدة برد"*).
- The quadriliteral root observation (z-m-h-r) is accurate; زمهرير is a quadriliteral.
- The abrār narrative (76:8–10), including *qamṭarīr*, is faithfully rendered.
