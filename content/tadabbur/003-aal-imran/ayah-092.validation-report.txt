ISSUES:
- [MODERATE] The entire reflection frames البر as "righteousness" — a spiritual quality — without engaging the dominant classical tafsir position that البر here specifically means الجنة (Paradise). Jalalayn: «أي ثوابه وهو الجنَّةُ»; Muyassar: «لن تدركوا الجنة»; Baghawi explicitly: «يعني : الجنة، قاله ابن عباس وابن مسعود ومجاهد». Baghawi does preserve minority views (Muqatil: taqwa; al-Hasan: being among the abrar), so "righteousness" is not fabricated — but rendering it as the settled meaning without acknowledging the majority position of three named Companions and tabi'in understates the classical consensus. The reflection's framing — "a chamber of righteousness," "the threshold of righteousness" — lands on the minority reading.
