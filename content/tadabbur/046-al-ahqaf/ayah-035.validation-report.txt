"Break faith" is a literary narrowing of *fisq* (stepping outside the bounds of obedience). It is not a mistranslation, but it is more specific than the lexicon supports and could mislead a reader into thinking *fasiq* here refers uniquely to oath-breakers rather than the generically disobedient.

The word means conveyance / a message conveyed; "delivery" is defensible from the root بلغ, but the classical sources (Muyassar: *هذا بلاغ لهم ولغيرهم*) frame it as "this is a conveyance for them and others" — i.e., a sufficient public declaration. "Clear" is not lexically present in بلاغ itself (that would be *mubīn*); the adjective appears to be inferred from context rather than the word. Not wrong, but slightly augmented.
