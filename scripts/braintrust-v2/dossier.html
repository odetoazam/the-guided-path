Scope: exact phrase قاتلهم الله انى يوفكون = {9:30, 63:4}, both verse-final; core pairing is Quran-only, idiom payoff flagged as tafsir.

You Shove the Orphan Away. The Same Verb Shoves You Into the Fire.

high-shareability (single-screenshot moral punch) + morphology-carries-meaning for the rigor readers
mechanismverb voice-flip census (root د-ع-ع, 2 verb tokens: 107:2 active / 52:13 passive)
verified receipt

Root د-ع-ع = 2 verb tokens only: 107:2 يَدُعُّ (active, 3rd-masc-SINGULAR) vs 52:13 يُدَعُّ (passive, 3rd-masc-PLURAL) — a voice flip AND a number shift; plus 1 verbal noun دَعًّا at 52:13. Distinct from the homograph دعو "to call" (212 tokens).

fillsThe voice-flip FORM is published (budge-root زحزح, line 6) but this CONTENT is new and morally sharper — the strongest untouched voice-flip case, zero Arabic prerequisite, a one-sentence WhatsApp forward. Fills the 'grammar itself carries the meaning' shareable unit with fresh, moral content.
corrections the writer must apply

1) MORPHOLOGY: not 'same aspect, only voice differs' — 107:2 is 3MS (singular active يَدُعُّ), 52:13 is 3MP (plural passive يُدَعّونَ). State active→passive voice flip WITH a singular→plural number shift (the single shover answered by the plural shoved). 2) 'THE SAME PEOPLE' / 'the hand that pushes becomes the body that is pushed' is a moral/contrapasso RESONANCE, not a Quranic identity claim — the subjects are textually distinct. Earn the link honestly via shared root كذب: both are deniers of the din (107:1 يكذب بالدين; 52:11 المكذبين; 52:14 كنتم بها تكذبون). Do not assert the Quran identifies the same individuals. 3) SCOPE: keep the 'as a verb' qualifier on 'only twice' — a 3rd token, verbal noun دَعًا at 52:13, exists. 4) Differentiate from the published زحزح voice-flip template (novelty is the moral content, not the form) and position so it does NOT duplicate the verified-pool BRACKET-10 use of 107:2 (يتم/كذب census). No folk-phonetics on the doubled ʿayn — the point is voice, not sound. 5) State the two facts and let the resonance show; do not claim the Quran 'designed' a rhyme; no superlatives.

The Angels Tell Ibrahim's Wife She'll Have a Son — She Laughs in One Surah and Slaps Her Own Face in Another

high-shareability (warm, human, woman-centered) + classical ikhtilaf recovery (dahikat)
mechanismcross-telling scene-reaction contrast (11:71 / 51:29) + hapax (sakkat, root ص-ك-ك)
verified receipt

أوجَسَ منهم خيفة is verbatim in both tellings (11:70, 51:28), anchoring the same annunciation scene. صَكَّت (she struck her face) is a true hapax — root ص-ك-ك occurs only at 51:29. ضَحِكَت (she laughed) is at 11:71. "Sarah" is extra-Quranic — use "Ibrahim's wife".

fillsCatalog is dense with woman-centered pieces — Maryam (multiple), Asiya, Bilqis, mother of Musa, women of Egypt — all reverent and theological. NONE shows a matriarch's raw, un-airbrushed startled reaction. Different register (warm, almost funny), high forwardability; fills the 'permission to be human' + non-solemn woman-centered gap.
corrections the writer must apply

1) Keep the dahikat 'laughed vs. menstruated' ikhtilaf visible in published copy; do not present 'she laughs' as the settled or only reading. 2) Reframe 'the laugh and the disbelief': incredulity is present in Hud too (11:72 'woe to me, shall I bear while I am an old woman'). The clean, corpus-grounded contrast is between two physical GESTURES preserved across the two tellings (she laughed / she struck her own face in a cry), NOT joy-in-one-surah vs. disbelief-in-the-other — do not imply Hud lacks shock. 3) If citing the ص-ر-ر root's 6 verses, do not imply all mean 'clamor' — 4 mean persist/be-obstinate, 1 (3:117) means cold wind; the clamor sense (صَرّة) is in fact unique to 51:29 — either drop the count or state the clamor sense itself is rare. 4) Keep صكك (sakkat, hapax at 51:29) and ضحك (present at 11:71) roots distinct in prose; ground the same-annunciation claim on أوجس منهم خيفة (verbatim in 11:70 and 51:28); 'Sarah' is extra-Quranic — keep the name out of the payoff, use 'Ibrahim's wife'. No superlatives.
