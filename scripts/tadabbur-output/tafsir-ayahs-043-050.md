## 44:43

### Ibn Kathir (English)

Verily, the tree of Zaqqu (43)Will be the food of the sinners (44)Like boiling oil, it will boil in the bellies (45)Like the boiling of scalding water (46)(It will be said:) "Seize him and drag him into the midst of blazing Fire, (47)"Then pour over his head the torment of boiling water. (48)"Taste you (this)! Verily, you were (pretending to be) the mighty, the generous. (49)"Verily, this is that whereof you used to doubt! (50)
