لَا تَحْزَنْ
Do not grieve.
He said it three times — to three people alone.
Hajar
alone in the desert, her infant dying of thirst
Maryam
alone in labor, under a palm tree
Muhammad ﷺ
in the cave, enemies searching outside
Same two words.
Three centuries apart.
He knows exactly where you are.
