Ad-Duḥā · 93:3
مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ
Your Lord has not
forsaken you.
AyahGuide
For days, no revelation came.
The sky went quiet.
And they mocked him:
“His Lord has abandoned him.”
Then this came down
مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ
“Your Lord has not bid you farewell,
nor does He detest—”
He kept “you” on the first verb — waddaʿa-ka.
وَمَا قَلَىٰ
But here, He dropped it.
Not qalā-ka. Just qalā.
He would not let the word “detest” touch you —
not even to say no.
وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَىٰ
And your Lord is going to keep giving to you —
until you are pleased.
