# The Book Backbone Map — What We Already Have

*The mission is not to write the book. It is to form its backbone from work already done —
mining the existing corpus (the guidance ebook, ~3,000 tadabbur files, 61 deep-analysis
articles, the surah analyses, the semantic graph) plus the new research substrate, and
mapping all of it onto the chapter structure. This document is that map.*

**Date:** 2026-07-22. Living document — the parallel corpus-mining agents' inventories fold
in below as they complete (§4–5). Built while forming the book's research backbone (not
drafting prose).

## 0. Scope note — this book stands on its own foundation

**The book's backbone is the research substrate + the corpus** (Azam's notes, the
waswasa/qadar/signs/machine-of-hidayah/same-rain research docs, the ~3,000 tadabbur files,
the 36 articles). That is the foundation, and it is substantial and independent. Everything
below §1 inventories *that*.

**On the ebook (a reference input, NOT the plan).** During the corpus survey a file surfaced
— `public/guides/architecture-of-guidance.html` (+ `.pdf`), "The Architecture of Guidance" —
which turns out to be Azam's own **subscriber lead-magnet**, committed 2026-04-12 and wired
into the subscribe-confirm + announce-guide email flows. It is a ~5,000-word accessible
distillation of the same guidance thesis. **Azam's steer (2026-07-22): he did not know it
existed, does not care about it as an artifact, and it is NOT the book — but it "might be
relevant."** So treat it as exactly that: a *possible* reference, useful for two concrete
things only —
1. **Proof the thesis is articulable for a layman** (it does exist in accessible prose), and
2. **Two citations worth verifying** it already names: Ibn Barrajān of Seville (two-books) and
   Ibn al-Qayyim's four movements of contemplation.

Do **not** treat it as the book's spine, its voice benchmark, or its outline authority. The
book's voice is Azam's to set; the outline is the symmetric-drama structure in the dossier.
The section-map below (§1) keeps the ebook only as a cross-reference column, not the driver.

### The ebook's own section sequence (its table of contents, in order)
1. **The Question Behind the Question** — the 17-times-a-day Fatiha hook; "if guidance were
   only information, you'd need it once."
2. **A World Within the World** — the determinate world; two people, same city, different
   realities; "the world your heart makes visible."
3. **The Self in Motion** — the nafs spectrum (ammāra ↔ muṭmaʾinna); khawāṭir; the feedback
   loop (state → which thoughts gain traction → actions → rebuilds the architecture).
4. **What Signs Are** — *āyah* = one word, two directions (scripture + nature); the
   doctor/bruise; "a sign only works if the observer can read it."
5. **How Guidance Operates** — guidance as *light* (makes terrain visible, doesn't move
   your feet); *sharḥ al-ṣadr* (39:22, the three layers); 7:146 (turned away from signs).
6. **The Two Books** — Quran + nature as two divine communications; **Ibn Barrajān of
   Seville** (12th-c. Andalusian: "reading the books of nature, revelation, and man").
7. **Preparing the Ground** — the five states as *qualities of soil*: **Need, Curiosity,
   Gratitude, Awe (khushūʿ), Purity**.
8. **The Practice of Contemplation** — *tafakkur / tadabbur / tadhakkur* distinguished;
   **Ibn al-Qayyim's four movements** (reflect on: highest purpose / the means / what pulls
   away / the signs); the heart as a *lens* that projects the light of meaning.
9. **Guidance as a Living Relationship** — the feedback loop both directions; guidance is a
   direction not a destination.
10. **Beginning** — call to action; start with Al-Fātiḥa.

**Interlocutors the ebook already surfaces** (these close several dossier `[needs sourcing]`
flags at a stroke — verify the exact references but the anchors are chosen):
- **Ibn Barrajān of Seville** (d. 1141) — the "two books / reading nature, revelation, and
  man" theme. A stronger, more classical anchor for the Signs chapter's articulate/
  inarticulate structure than the Nursi letter/noun note (dossier B.6). *Use as primary;
  keep Nursi as the modern echo.*
- **Ibn al-Qayyim's four movements of contemplation** — already articulated; this is the
  B.1 Ibn Qayyim material, partially in hand. (Still verify the exact locus — Miftāḥ Dār

## 3. Backbone principle (how the pieces combine into a chapter)

Each chapter is built in four layers, all of which already largely exist:
1. **Spine** — the ebook section (voice + narrative arc). *Exists.*
2. **Depth/receipts** — the research doc (corpus census + four-mufassir tafsir). *Exists for
   2½/3/4/5/6; needed for 7 and the 1/2/8 consolidations.*
3. **Reservoir** — the 15-25 richest existing tadabbur files supplying worked examples,
   linguistic detail, and per-ayah depth. *Exists; being mapped in §4.*
4. **Set-pieces** — the deep-analysis articles that can become a chapter's centerpiece
   passage (e.g. the guidance-seam or forgetting/sight articles). *Exists; being mapped in
   §5.*

The Fable prose draft (later) works from all four layers at once. **No chapter starts from a
blank page** — each starts from an ebook section already in the right voice, and the job is
expansion + receipting, not invention.

### ⚠ CRITICAL methodological catch (from the mining)
**The concept-census in §2 UNDERCOUNTS.** The newer, often richest tadabbur files (e.g. the
entire nafs-spectrum trilogy 12:53 / 75:2 / 89:27-30) use a *morphology-comment frontmatter
with NO `concepts:` field* — so every concept-grep misses them. §2's counts are a floor, not
a census. **Standing rule for all future corpus mining: search by ayah/theme AND by concept
tag; concept-grep alone silently drops the newest cohort.** (This is the fourth
encoding/indexing trap in the project — cf. the three hamza-root traps.)
